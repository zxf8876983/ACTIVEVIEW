## 1. Executive Summary & Research Objective

In active robot perception, a mobile robot must autonomously navigate to an optimal observation viewpoint to overcome visual obstructions (environmental furniture occlusion, anatomical self-occlusion, and perspective distortion), thereby maximizing 3D pose estimation fidelity and action recognition accuracy.

**ACTIVEVIEW v11.5** establishes a rigorous, scientific, zero-leakage benchmark:
1. **Zero Ground-Truth Joint Leakage**: ST-GCN **never** receives ground truth AMASS joint coordinates during training or inference. The entire perception pipeline strictly adheres to:
   $$\text{AMASS Motion} \longrightarrow \text{SMPL Mesh} \longrightarrow \text{RGB Image} \longrightarrow \text{Keypoint R-CNN} \longrightarrow \text{VideoPose3D} \longrightarrow \text{Skeleton Normalizer} \longrightarrow \text{ST-GCN}$$
2. **Strict Instance-Level Motion Partitioning**: Motion instances from AMASS are partitioned into disjoint sets ($\text{Train}: 70\%, \text{Val}: 15\%, \text{Test}: 15\%$) with rigorous verification:
   $$\text{Train} \cap \text{Val} = \emptyset, \quad \text{Train} \cap \text{Test} = \emptyset, \quad \text{Val} \cap \text{Test} = \emptyset$$
3. **Frozen Action Model During Test**: ST-GCN is trained exclusively on estimated skeletons under clean perception studio environments and completely frozen during active perception evaluation.
4. **Physical Occlusion Degradation**: Shannon entropy changes strictly emerge from genuine sensory degradation (visual occlusion $\to$ 2D keypoint drop/shift $\to$ 3D skeleton deformation $\to$ ST-GCN probability dispersion).

## 3. Clean Perception ST-GCN Training

### 3.1 Studio Generation & Normalization
- **Environment**: Empty Habitat Studio (`backend_cfg.scene_id = "NONE"`), 100% solid background.
- **Perception Model**: Keypoint R-CNN ResNet-50 FPN on CUDA $\to$ VideoPose3D `TemporalModel` (`pretrained_h36m_detectron_coco.bin`) $\to$ Human3.6M 17 joints.
- **Normalization Pipeline**:
  1. Pelvis Root Centering: $p_i(t) \leftarrow p_i(t) - p_{\text{pelvis}}(t)$;
  2. Torso Scale Normalization: $p_i(t) \leftarrow p_i(t) / s$;
  3. Canonical Frame Alignment: Coronal/Sagittal orthogonal basis projection.
- **Generated Samples**:
  - `clean_perception_v11_5/train`: $(N=160, C=3, T=30, V=17, M=1)$
  - `clean_perception_v11_5/val`: $(N=60, C=3, T=30, V=17, M=1)$

### 3.2 ST-GCN Training Convergence
- **Topology**: Spatial Partitioning ($K=3$) adjacency matrix on 17-joint Human3.6M graph.
- **Optimization**: Adam ($\text{lr}=10^{-3}$, $\text{weight\_decay}=10^{-4}$), Cosine Annealing, 60 Epochs.
- **Results**:
  - **Best Validation Accuracy**: **55.00%** (multi-class clean perception without test leakage)
  - **Saved Checkpoint**: `checkpoints/v11_5/stgcn_v11_5_best.pth` (**FROZEN**)

## 4. Habitat Active View Benchmark Evaluation

### 4.1 Evaluation Protocol
- **Scene**: Photorealistic HM3D household residence (`00800-TEEsavR23oF` living room with sofas, armchairs, and stairs).
- **Physical Contact**: Downward physical raycasting with $4.5\text{cm}$ sole compensation achieving $100\%$ zero-gap floor contact.
- **Candidate Viewpoint Pool**: $N=32$ candidate viewpoints ($r \in [1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0]\text{m}$, $\theta \in [0^\circ, 45^\circ, \dots, 315^\circ]$).
- **Tested Motions**: Strictly drawn from `datasets/amass_split/test.json`.
